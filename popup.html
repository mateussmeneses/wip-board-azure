Required only when special rule is enabled.

Special people rule: for each configured person, all cards matching the special tag prefix count as 1 block in WIP (instead of counting each card separately).

ALERT THRESHOLDS

Aging rule: when a card reaches the configured warning/high/critical aging thresholds, visual card alarms are applied based on severity.

Target date rule: when a card has Target Date with remaining days less than or equal to the configured threshold, it is marked as alert. Overdue cards are also alerted.
